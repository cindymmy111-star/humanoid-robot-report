Tesla's Optimus has the most capital and buzz, but its actual production timeline lags expectations. Overall, the industry is shifting from "lab demos" to competing on real-world orders, with factories and logistics scaling first and the home segment still in early adoption.

核心技术与产业链Core Technology & Supply Chain

硬件BOM约占整机成本60%–70%,关节模组(电机+减速器+丝杠)是成本与技术壁垒最集中的环节;"大脑"层面则是VLA具身大模型的路线竞速。Hardware BOM accounts for roughly 60–70% of unit cost, with joint modules (motors + reducers + lead screws) concentrating both cost and technical barriers; at the "brain" layer, the race is between VLA-based embodied AI models.
